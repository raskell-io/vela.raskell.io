Vela

Vela deploys your apps to a server you own, with no downtime and no containers. It is one Rust binary: the daemon on the server, the client on your laptop, SSH in between.

It is built at raskell.io, next to Zentinel and Refrakt, and it deploys the apps behind Cyanea and Archipelag.io.
